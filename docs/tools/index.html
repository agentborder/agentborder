    Why not just block everything? Training crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot and friends) take your content to train models and send you no visitors. AI-answer and search crawlers (OAI-SearchBot, Perplexity, Googlebot) are how people find you through AI and search. Block all and your bill drops but so does your traffic. This list keeps that line drawn for you.
    

      
    One catch: robots.txt is a polite request. Well-behaved crawlers obey it, bad ones ignore it. For crawlers that ignore robots.txt, use the Cloudflare, nginx, or Apache rule, which actually enforces at the server or edge. New crawlers appear constantly, so a hand-kept list rots.
